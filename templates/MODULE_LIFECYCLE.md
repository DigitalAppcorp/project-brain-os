# MODULE LIFECYCLE

## Gate 0 — Idea
Capture what might exist.

## Gate 1 — Value
Who needs it, what problem/desire, what outcome, why now.

## Gate 2 — Cost & dependencies
Frontend, backend, security, moderation, APIs, operations, privacy, network effects.

## Gate 2.5 — Idea Bank + Conceptual Audit
Let the Product Owner dump ideas freely. Audit utility, engagement, acquisition, monetization, cost, dependencies, safety and MVP fit.

## Gate 3 — Cheapest honest experiment
Use a fake door/prototype/manual test when it can answer the uncertainty honestly.
Use a **minimum useful real core** when the module is central to the MVP and a placeholder would produce misleading evidence or damage the product experience.

## Gate 4 — Evidence
Measure unique users, conversion, revisit, intent, source and relevant behavior.

## Gate 5 — Investment decision
BUILD NOW / MVP REDUCED / ACTIVE EXPERIMENT / POSTPONED.

## Gate 6 — Product specification
Exact scope, flows, roles, permissions, states, privacy, metrics and DoD.

## Gate 7 — Technical architecture
Data, ownership, authorization/RLS, APIs, storage, indexes, concurrency, migrations and tests.

## Gate 8 — Implementation
Branch, versioned backend, build, tests, review, approval where required, merge.

## Gate 9 — Post-launch validation
Adoption, recurrence, retention, errors, operations and next investment.
